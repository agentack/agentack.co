## 1. Positioning — locked

| Element | Decision |
|---|---|
| **Category** | AI lead conversion systems and custom software for real estate |
| **Wedge** | AI-powered real estate lead conversion |
| **Primary ICP** | Established brokerages and agencies in the UAE (5–200 agents) |
| **Secondary ICP** | Property developers, especially off-plan launches |
| **Third ICP** | Property management and leasing teams |
| **Markets** | UAE first; Saudi Arabia and Qatar next; wider GCC selectively |
| **Primary CTA** | **Book a discovery call** |
| **Secondary CTA** | See what we build |
| **Descriptor** | AI and software for real estate lead conversion |
| **Homepage H1** | **Turn property enquiries into booked viewings.** |

### The one-sentence position

> Agentack builds AI lead conversion systems and custom software for brokerages and developers in the UAE and wider GCC — capturing, qualifying and routing every enquiry, with a human in the loop when it matters.

### Messaging hierarchy

1. **Outcome** — more of the leads you already pay for become viewings.
2. **Mechanism** — capture, qualify, route, book, follow up.
3. **Depth** — custom software and integrations behind the wedge.
4. **Risk reversal** — free discovery, paid assessment, 2–4 week pilot, written scope.
5. **CTA** — book a discovery call.

### What we deliberately do not say

- No client logos, testimonials or named case studies.
- No “trusted by”, “500+ clients”, invented conversion percentages or response-time claims.
- No claim of a UAE/Saudi office, local entity or boots on the ground.
- No claim of in-house Arabic expertise. Arabic is built with native review before launch.
- No claim of GDPR/UAE PDPL/Saudi PDPL compliance, certifications or guaranteed data residency.
- No guarantee of revenue, sales, conversion or rankings.

The site handles this with a **Proof, honestly** section: working demo, architecture, pilot report and code handover. That is the credibility strategy until real results exist.

## 2. Offer architecture

```
ENTRY     Free discovery (30 min)          → map the flow, decide fit
ASSESS    Technical assessment $250–$500   → written scope, architecture, price
PROVE     Pilot from $750, 2–4 weeks       → one workflow on real enquiries
BUILD     Production $3,000–$10,000+       → multi-channel, CRM, dashboards
SUPPORT   $300–$1,500/month, 3-month min   → monitoring, fixes, optimisation
```

Adjacent work: custom CRM, portals, websites, internal tools, SaaS, mobile (case by case), integrations, dashboards.

**Commercial rules shown on the site:**

- Assessment fee credited toward implementation if the client proceeds.
- Small projects 50/50; larger 50/30/20; retainers in advance.
- Third-party API, cloud and messaging costs billed separately.
- Quoted in USD; AED and SAR available.

- Real estate vocabulary: enquiry, viewing, listing, brokerage, off-plan, tenant, owner, handover, escrow, Ejari.
- No hype: no “revolutionary”, “10x”, “AI workforce”, “game-changing”.

### Arabic

Arabic is **not** live. The layout, fonts (`Noto Naskh Arabic`, `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`) and RTL rules are in place, but the language switch was removed rather than faked. Before Arabic launches:

1. Commission a native Gulf-Arabic reviewer.
2. Rewrite headlines for Arabic; do not translate literally.
3. Review every claim against the English.
4. Add `hreflang` and localized metadata.
5. Reinstate a working language switch with a real route or locale segment.

## 7. What is built

- 13 routes, all static, all returning 200.
- Shared content model in `lib/content.ts`.
- Reusable components for heroes, problem framing, service ladder, personas, pricing, proof, specialisms, process, FAQ, integrations, pilot, CTA and contact.
- Contact form posts to `app/api/contact` with validation, honeypot, timing trap, payload cap and per-IP rate limiting; delivery through Resend and/or HubSpot from environment variables.
- Optional Cal.com booking section on `/contact`, shown only when `NEXT_PUBLIC_CALCOM_LINK` is set.
- Working light/dark themes, focus rings, reduced-motion support, RTL-ready CSS.
- Organization JSON-LD, per-page metadata and canonicals, sitemap and robots.
- Honest positioning: no fake proof, no fake Arabic switch, no fake office, no guaranteed results.

## 8. Launch gaps — in priority order

1. **Form delivery credentials.** The handler at `app/api/contact` is built: validation, honeypot, timing trap, payload cap, per-IP rate limit and Resend + HubSpot delivery. Add `RESEND_API_KEY` (or HubSpot form credentials) in `.env.local`, then send one real test enquiry. Returns `503` until a provider is configured — no fake success.
2. **Cal.com link.** The booking section is built and renders when `NEXT_PUBLIC_CALCOM_LINK` is set. Create the event type, add the link, and test the embed on mobile.
3. **Contact email.** Confirm `contact@agentack.co` is live and monitored.
4. **Analytics.** GA4 + GTM with consent; events for CTA clicks, form start/submit, calendar open, WhatsApp click, FAQ open and scroll depth.
5. **Demo asset.** Record a 45–90 second walkthrough of a lead-conversion flow. The “Proof, honestly” section becomes much stronger with it.
6. **Logo SVG.** Replace the transparent PNG with the vector so it is crisp at every size and works on dark panels.
7. **Legal review.** Privacy and terms are solid drafts, not legal advice.
8. **Insights.** Publish the first original piece (e.g. a response-time audit of Gulf brokerages) before adding an insights hub.
9. **Case study.** Once a pilot completes, add `/results` and one anonymised or named case study.
10. **Arabic.** Only after a native reviewer is engaged.
