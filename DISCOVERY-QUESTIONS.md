# My additional strategic recommendations

There are three decisions I'd particularly emphasize before finalizing the website.

## 1. Don't make Agentack look like an AI chatbot agency

our positioning should be:

Agentack builds intelligent technology infrastructure for modern real estate businesses.

That gives you room to sell:

- AI agents.
- Lead management systems.
- Custom CRMs.
- Property management software.
- Multi-agent systems.
- Internal business platforms.
- SaaS development.

You're not restricting yourself to one automation.

## 2. Build your website around three service categories

I would organize the services into:

| Category             | What you sell                                                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AI Solutions         | AI agents, lead qualification, intelligent follow-ups, multi-agent systems |
| Real Estate Software | CRMs, property platforms, dashboards, custom SaaS                          |
| Business Automation  | WhatsApp workflows, integrations, sales operations, internal automation    |

This creates a clear connection between your AI engineering and MERN/software development capabilities.

## 3. Target established brokerages, not individual agents

This is particularly important for your $5,000+ implementation goal.

An individual property agent might need a chatbot. An established brokerage might need an entire lead management and conversion infrastructure.

Those are very different purchasing decisions.

Your website should speak to the second buyer.

One important distinction: I would not advertise Agentack as an established GCC real estate technology provider yet. Position it as a specialist technology partner with a clear solution, strong engineering capabilities, and demonstrable working systems. That is ambitious without making claims you cannot substantiate.
