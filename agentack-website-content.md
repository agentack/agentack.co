Software and AI for Gulf real estate. English copy with key Arabic lines.

**How to read this file**
- `[BRACKETS]` mark facts only you can supply: prices, contact details, timelines, integrations. Nothing in brackets has been invented.
- Items marked **(publish only when real)** describe things that should not go live until they exist.

---

## 0. Voice and style rules

- **Calm, specific, confident.** No hype words: avoid "revolutionary", "10x", "AI workforce", "without headcount".
- **Headlines are short fragments**, often in a "Label. Phrase." shape. Example: *Clear scope. Practical next steps.*
- **Body copy is one sentence per item.** Lists come in threes where possible.
- **Eyebrow labels** sit above most section headings, in uppercase (English only).
- **Hedge anything regulated or commercial.** Use "we confirm scope in the diagnostic" rather than promising outcomes or compliance.
- **No numbers without a source.** No client logos, no testimonials, no statistics until they are real.
- Say "enquiry", "viewing", "listing", "brokerage", "off-plan". Use the vocabulary the buyer uses.
